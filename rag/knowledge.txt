Home Credit India - Loan Against Property (LAP) Knowledge Base

[SECTION: OVERVIEW & OFFER]
Home Credit offers pre-approved Loan Against Property (LAP) of up to ₹75 Lakh (₹75,00,000) for existing customers. LAP allows property owners to unlock the hidden value in their residential, commercial, or industrial properties to fulfill business, personal, or financial goals.

[SECTION: ELIGIBILITY & CRITERIA]
- Maximum Loan Amount: ₹75,00,000 (₹75 Lakh).
- Eligible Property Types: Residential houses/flats, Commercial shops/offices, and Industrial properties with clear, marketable title.
- Ineligible Property Types: Agricultural land/farms are STRICTLY ineligible.
- Ownership: Both Sole ownership and Joint ownership are eligible.
- Original Documents: Original property title deed and registration documents must be available for verification.
- Income & Employment: Salaried employees and Self-employed individuals/business owners are eligible.
- Mode of Income: Income MUST be received through Bank account (salary credit, bank deposit, cheque). Cash income is NOT accepted.
- Property Market Value: Customer's estimated current market value is captured to calculate Loan-to-Value (LTV). There is no arbitrary minimum value restriction.
- Tenure Range: Minimum tenure is 3 years (36 months), Maximum tenure is 15 years (180 months).

[SECTION: INTEREST RATES & PRICING]
- Interest Rate Policy: Home Credit offers competitive floating interest rates tailored to customer credit score, property evaluation, and profile.
- Exact Interest Rate: The exact interest rate is determined after technical evaluation and credit risk assessment. It will be communicated directly by our Senior Loan Expert during the final application process.

[SECTION: DOCUMENTS REQUIRED]
- Identity Proof: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID.
- Address Proof: Aadhaar Card, Passport, Utility Bill.
- Property Documents: Original Title Deed, Sale Deed, Property Tax Receipts, Approved Building Plan.
- Income Proof: Last 6 months bank account statements, salary slips (for salaried) or ITR returns & P&L statements (for self-employed).

[SECTION: USAGE & END USES]
- Business Expansion & Working Capital
- Debt Consolidation & Refinancing
- Children Higher Education or Marriage
- Home Renovation & Medical Emergencies

[SECTION: BALANCE TRANSFER & EXISTING LOANS]
- Customers who already have an existing property loan or wish to reduce their current monthly EMI can apply for Home Credit Balance Transfer.
- Dedicated Loan Transfer Specialists manage balance transfer requests to offer EMI optimization.
